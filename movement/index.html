Benin

View issue

Regenerative social DeFi fundraising platform

View issue

Problem

Grassroots projects in Benin struggle to access funding due to low trust in traditional charity platforms, limited transparency, and a focus on short-term donations.

Solution

Build a regenerative fundraising platform where users support local causes through yield-generating staking, transparent governance, and community-driven funding allocation.

Stack

React, Node.js, Aave/Morpho, Logos Messaging, Status, tokenised RWAs, DAO governance, and privacy-preserving identity.
