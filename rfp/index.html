How it works

Five steps from spec to payout

How it works.

Each RFP is an open request for a protocol or building block the whole
ecosystem can use. Your team proposes how to build it. Selected teams
get a grant, paid per milestone, so you obtain funding to gain Logos
stack expertise.

  1. 01

    Read the spec.

    Each RFP sets the scope, the
    requirements, the team profile and
    the expected timeline.

  2. 02

    Submit a proposal.

    Submit a proposal via the dedicated
    form with your team, technical
    approach, milestones, and budget.

  3. 03

    Get a decision.

    Expect a decision within 14 days of
    the RFP closing. Logos may ask
    questions or suggest changes.

  4. 04

    Sign a grant
    agreement.

    Milestones, payouts, and the timeline
    are set in writing.

  5. 05

    Build and get paid.

    Each milestone is paid as it lands.

You propose the milestones. You set the budget.

Break the work into milestones, each with its own
deliverables, USD payout, and duration.

Handling user funds or new cryptography? Add an audit
milestone.

What you ship

What is included in every funded build.

The bar for RFPs is purposefully high as other teams will
be building on the infrastructure you create.

  1. 01

    A module or SDK other Logos apps can call

  2. 02

    A mini-app that runs in Basecamp

  3. 03

    A CLI for core operations

  4. 04

    Tests in CI, green on the default branch

  5. 05

    Docs for developers and for users

  6. 06

    A privacy document: what observers see, what stays private, what's trusted

  7. 07

    A design decision log

  8. 08

    Open source code under MIT and Apache 2.0

Who should apply

Who these specs are written for.

Teams that want to grow with Logos, not deliver and leave. Each RFP
names the experience it needs: custody design, Rust for zkVM targets,
applied cryptography, DeFi protocol engineering.

You build complex software and get paid as each milestone lands.
During this process, you'll cultivate deep expertise in the Logos stack
and join a community and ecosystem building for the long term.

The terms

The terms.

Read the full terms
  1. 01

    You keep ownership of your code. It is licensed open source under MIT and Apache 2.0.

  2. 02

    Proposals are public.

  3. 03

    No grant is awarded until a grant agreement is signed.

  4. 04

    Logos is the grantor. It doesn't deploy, host or operate what you build.

  5. 05

    Logos selects at its discretion and may select no proposal.
