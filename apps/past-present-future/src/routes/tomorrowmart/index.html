How these prices are calculated

The rate we use

Every price on this site compounds at the same annual rate: your country's latest published inflation rate. Nothing is weighted, nothing is exaggerated.

How it adds up each year

Price in year N = today's shelf price × (1 + rate)N. The same arithmetic a pension forecast uses.
