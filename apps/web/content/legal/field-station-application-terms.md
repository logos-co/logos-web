## 2. Introduction to the Programme

1. The Programme, also referred to as “Field Station”, is a one-week residency programme at a 500-acre regenerative project in Rajasthan, where accepted Applicants will come together to create a real, working parallel society: living, eating, debugging, and shipping solutions to real-world issues.

   The Programme takes place in collaboration with Zu-Grama, an experimental initiative focused on developing pro-human places and technologies and exploring new forms of community, coordination and collective living. Zu-Grama is operated and administered by C’est La Vie Sport LLP, an entity independent of Logos

   Residents will choose one of the four tracks listed below to build a prototype. At the end of the one-week programme, a demo day will give participants the opportunity to showcase their work for a chance to win milestone-based grants to continue the project.

   Additional details can be found on [https://logos.co/field-station](https://logos.co/field-station).
