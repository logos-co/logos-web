3e:T509a,<h2 id="cultivating-community">Cultivating community</h2><p>In the last&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/april-2025">State of the Logos Network</a>, we spoke of ramping up our efforts to build a strong, aligned, and highly participatory community around our&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">vision for the future of human governance</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>We’re already starting to see the fruits of these initiatives, as community members have stepped up and started to contribute on various fronts.
