f:T509a,<h2 id="cultivating-community">Cultivating community</h2><p>In the last&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/april-2025">State of the Logos Network</a>, we spoke of ramping up our efforts to build a strong, aligned, and highly participatory community around our&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">vision for the future of human governance</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>We’re already starting to see the fruits of these initiatives, as community members have stepped up and started to contribute on various fronts. This is just the beginning. We need more of you to bring your creativity, passion, and technical skills to the cause.&nbsp;</p><p>There are ways to get involved in the “<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.com/channels/973324189794697286/1372894505396867082/1373989785714163746">Contribute</a>” channel in Discord, or you can post ideas for initiatives on the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://forum.logos.co/">Logos Forum</a>.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="logos-campaigns">Logos Campaigns</h3><p><strong>Save the Songs: Decentralise the Internet Archive’s Music Collection</strong><br>The Logos community rallied behind the Internet Archive at the end of May. The digital library is facing a $621 million lawsuit from major record labels over its efforts to digitise increasingly rare music. Logos stands with the Internet Archive and all similar efforts to make knowledge universally accessible.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="/media/article/save-the-songs">Support the campaign now</a>.</p><p><img alt="IA_Article cover Image 1920x1080-upd.png" src="/media-images/IA_Article_cover_Image_1920x1080_upd_f37af272bd-8b92785a-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/IA_Article_cover_Image_1920x1080_upd_f37af272bd-8b92785a-750.webp 750w, /media-images/IA_Article_cover_Image_1920x1080_upd_f37af272bd-8b92785a-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h3 id="logos-irl">Logos IRL</h3><p><strong>Logos Circles</strong><br>Logos Circles are a major new way for us to bridge the digital divide, make personal connections, and grow our tribe. We’re bringing our ideas and technologies to folks on the ground via various pop-up hubs around the world and hoping the community will spin up new Logos Circles of their own.</p><p>The&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://lu.ma/LCLisbon1">first Logos Circle</a> was in Lisbon, Portugal, on 4 June. A crowd of more than 25 occupied The Block Lisboa, including locals, the city’s expat community, and those from further afield looking to pirate ideas for future Logos Circles back home.</p><p>After a lively discussion, several winnable issues emerged: how can we increase the number of green spaces in Lisbon and protect them from invasive species? How can we build a verifiable news source for the people of Lisbon to stop the epidemic of fake news influencing people's choices and opinions?</p><p>The meetup also inspired us to unite with an existing project to start improving the lives of real people. On Sunday 8 June, we’ll be joining Arroze Studios’ weekly Digital Detox Sessions to aid their local maintenance and construction projects. If you’re in Lisbon this weekend, come show your support.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DKelpJRIpmm/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link">More info</a>.</p><p>We already have the second Circle planned for Brno, Czech Republic, on 12 June.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://lu.ma/LCBrno1">Register your interest now</a>.</p><p><img alt="logos-may-brno-circle.jpg" src="/media-images/logos_may_brno_circle_48095e36c8-05ac9bd1-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_may_brno_circle_48095e36c8-05ac9bd1-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_may_brno_circle_48095e36c8-05ac9bd1-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>A Logos Circle meetup in Los Angeles, California, is also in the early planning phase for a still-to-be-confirmed date. Keep your eyes on&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.gg/PqCYTnqdtN">the Discord channel</a> for more information.&nbsp;</p><p>If you want to help us spread our message and tech via your own local Logos Circle,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.gg/logosnetwork">hop into Discord</a> and let us know.
