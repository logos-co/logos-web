b:T5b20,<h2 id="raising-awareness-about-the-uk-s-mounting-debt-interest">Raising awareness about the UK’s mounting debt interest</h2><p>In June, Logos launched a new campaign to raise awareness of the scale of the UK’s interest payments on debt and how they’re hindering efficient governance.</p><p>In 2026, the UK government is set to spend £114 billion on interest on debt alone. That money pays for nothing new. It doesn’t build homes, fund hospitals, improve schools, repair roads, or even reduce the national debt. It’s the cost of servicing decisions already made, passed on to people who had no say in them.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:1200/630;" alt="uk-debt-interest-logos-june-update.png" src="/media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-750.webp 750w, /media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1200" height="630" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><p>This is a social problem as well as a fiscal one. As in almost all countries worldwide, spending on debt interest rather than core public priorities is causing the UK to lose the ability to invest in its own future.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/ukdebt">Check out the campaign homepage for more information.</a></p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.com/channels/973324189794697286/1435273609940832428">If you’re local, join Logos Circle / London to learn how to support the campaign.</a></p><h2 id="supporting-internet-freedom-with-internet-archive-and-funding-the-commons">Supporting internet freedom with Internet Archive and Funding the Commons&nbsp;&nbsp;</h2><p>In June, we concluded a campaign led by Tor and Funding the Commons. The goal was to support projects building tools and protocols to defend internet freedom, including SecureDrop, OnionShare, OONI, Onion Browser, and others.&nbsp;</p><p>As part of our support, Logos contributed to a matched funding pool alongside Cake Wallet, Zcash Community Grants, and Octant. Funding was allocated using a quadratic mechanism, ensuring that projects with the broadest community support received the most.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://internetfreedom.torproject.org/">Although the campaign has officially ended, you can still make donations via BTC, ETH, ZEC, XMR, and GLM here.</a></p><h2 id="can-logos-run-doom">Can Logos run DOOM?</h2><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:1030/767;" alt="logos-update-june-freedoom.png" src="/media-images/logos_update_june_freedoom_0704eb35ae-ad20419f-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_update_june_freedoom_0704eb35ae-ad20419f-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_update_june_freedoom_0704eb35ae-ad20419f-1030.webp 1030w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1030" height="767" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>FREEDOOM running in Logos Basecamp.</figcaption></figure><p>We all know that the ultimate test of a hardware or software stack is “Can it run DOOM?”</p><p>Well, thanks to the efforts of cyber monk Rahul, we can proudly declare that the Logos stack can indeed run DOOM (or more accurately, the free and open-source version of the game, FREEDOOM).</p><p>This is DOOM running inside Basecamp as a real, installable&nbsp;.lgx module that shows up as a tab in the sidebar. It uses doomgeneric, derived from id Software’s original open-source DOOM code, together with FreeDOOM’s fully free game assets, so no proprietary DOOM data is used.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/the-cybermonk/can-it-run-doom">Check the repo and run the DOOM module on your own Logos Basecamp instance now.</a> If you haven’t already started experimenting with Basecamp,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/logos-co/logos-basecamp/releases/tag/0.1.2">download and run it here.</a>&nbsp;</p><p>Build something interesting for Basecamp, let us know, and we’ll feature you in the next monthly update.&nbsp;</p><h2 id="irl-upcoming">IRL / Upcoming</h2><h3 id="dweb-camp">DWeb Camp</h3><p>Logos will host a series of pop-up activations at this year’s DWeb Camp in Alte Hölle, Germany.
