3b:T5b20,<h2 id="raising-awareness-about-the-uk-s-mounting-debt-interest">Raising awareness about the UK’s mounting debt interest</h2><p>In June, Logos launched a new campaign to raise awareness of the scale of the UK’s interest payments on debt and how they’re hindering efficient governance.</p><p>In 2026, the UK government is set to spend £114 billion on interest on debt alone. That money pays for nothing new. It doesn’t build homes, fund hospitals, improve schools, repair roads, or even reduce the national debt. It’s the cost of servicing decisions already made, passed on to people who had no say in them.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:1200/630;" alt="uk-debt-interest-logos-june-update.png" src="/media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-750.webp 750w, /media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1200" height="630" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><p>This is a social problem as well as a fiscal one.
