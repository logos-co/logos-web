3b:T6e26,<h1 id="building-a-movement">Building a movement</h1><p>Logos is a social movement.</p><p>Our mission: To revive civil society and defend fundamental freedoms.</p><p>Our means: A private-by-default technology stack designed to support applications and institutions that can’t be censored or surveilled. An aligned and committed community develops, builds on, and supports tools for liberation, deployed on the stack. The community is the beating heart of everything we do.</p><p>If you think that peer cooperation is a more effective engine for change than waiting for self-serving systems to reform themselves, get involved. Contribute your skills, insights, and enthusiasm – we support and recognise everyone making this journey with us.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://contribute.logos.co/en/">Submit proposed contributions through the Logos Contribute portal</a>, and we’ll help you get started.</p><h1 id="logos-unifies">Logos unifies</h1><p>We’ve been listening to community feedback over recent months. You told us our structure was hard to follow, which fragmented the community’s energy and created a barrier to contributing.&nbsp;We heard you, and we’ve made it our top priority to fix it.</p><p>In November, we revealed a new, consolidated structure. The Logos technology stack is now one unified identity, comprising storage (previously Codex), messaging (previously Waku), and blockchain (previously Nomos) components.</p><p>As part of the consolidation, we’ll be winding down websites, community servers, and social accounts for Nomos, Waku, and Codex. Going forward, all our cultural and movement-related announcements will come through the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">main Logos X account</a>, while technical updates will be via the new&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_devs">Logos Tech X account</a>. Our unified community hub and meeting point will be the Logos server (<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.gg/logosnetwork">clearnet</a> /&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://status.app/c/G54AAMT_mNvE4zy-g2YaEkeilMmfS6VkdHLA_mMlYUgBhmHigbUFbsPj85NIWPy2a39WE791XPf6j1VG_1baf_T1RB2lcYVg9anrB2-gAzzkVoAQRCwUouTqHCgYwBl7e1iX_RuGPlYUDKcQ6URT576hLh3Wk3vcyNvH_XV5f3a8xYg=#zQ3shhxxHeXxc1jb72h6LK6jSz4HfNLELYBKyhaDfZRji8cMa">private</a>).</p><h2 id="new-visual-identity">New visual identity</h2><p>The consolidation gave us the chance to rethink our design language, too. We’re still highly aligned with the cypherpunk ethos that our former stripped-back and brutalist look conveyed, but we wanted our visual identity to better represent the optimism we feel around our movement and technologies.</p><p>Our new design language is more aspirational and community oriented. It says:&nbsp;</p><blockquote><p>“We don’t need to fight. We already have the tools to peacefully resist tyranny and governmental overreach. Come operate, improve, and use them with us.”&nbsp;</p></blockquote><p>We’ve overhauled the entire Logos website to align it with our new direction;&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">check it out</a>.
