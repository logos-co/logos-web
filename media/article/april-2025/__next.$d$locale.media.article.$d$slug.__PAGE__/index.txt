10:T2d74,<h2 id="cultivating-community">Cultivating community</h2><p>Community is at the heart of everything we do at Logos because a committed, value-aligned community is essential to our vision of building a network state. This year, we’re doubling down on creating a truly open-source community with several initiatives described below and many more that we’ll reveal soon.&nbsp;</p><p>Open source is not only about having transparent code. It means involving the community in all aspects of Logos – from development to advocacy. We face an uphill battle, and we cannot succeed without your help.&nbsp;</p><p>Future State of the Logos Network editions will contain more details on how you can support our mission in more practical ways, but for now,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.gg/logosnetwork">join the community Discord</a>, introduce yourself, get involved in the discussions and share what you’re working on, or even just post a few memes.</p><h3 id="events">Events</h3><h4 id="ift-annual-meetup"><strong>IFT annual meetup</strong></h4><p>Contributors from across the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://free.technology/">Institute of Free Technology</a> met in Croatia at the end of April to strategise and collaborate. The time spent together deepened connections, aligned our work, and gave a rare opportunity to advance cross-project initiatives, such as&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://qaku.app/q/90e718656017">Qaku</a> and decentralising OpChan.&nbsp;</p><p>At the event, we saw a preview of Logos Core, an open modular platform architecture for the Logos ecosystem that will allow applications to easily import and start building on the technology stack. Logos projects also had the opportunity to showcase the significant strides they had made in protocol development. These presentations included a live demo of an early testnet version of the Nomos blockchain.</p><p>Expect to see the fruits of this collaboration in future State of the Logos Network updates.</p><p><img alt="logos-jarrad-keynote.png" src="/media-images/logos_jarrad_keynote_d188cd0f6d-a2c5ced9-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_jarrad_keynote_d188cd0f6d-a2c5ced9-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_jarrad_keynote_d188cd0f6d-a2c5ced9-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1999" height="1333" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h4 id="zuitzerland"><strong>Zuitzerland</strong></h4><p>Immediately after the IFT meetup, Logos representatives headed straight to&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://zuitzerland.ch/">Zuitzerland</a>, a pop-up city initiative hosted by contributors from Zuzalu in Switzerland. The event is an opportunity for Logos to expand our community by meeting fellow parallel society builders in person, strengthening existing bonds and forging new ones.&nbsp;</p><h4 id="parallel-society-festival"><strong>Parallel Society Festival</strong></h4><p>Planning for next year’s Parallel Society Festival is ongoing. The event, which debuted as Parallel Society Congress last year, provides a fresh take on the conference format with our coalition members co-curating a uniquely cultural experience. Alongside the usual presentations, panels, and workshops, you can expect live music from local and international talent, art exhibitions, theatre, and much more. We’re calling it an (un)conference because that’s exactly what it is!&nbsp;</p><p>We’ll reveal more about the next Parallel Society Festival, including the location, very soon – watch this space.</p><p><img alt="logos-april-psf.png" src="/media-images/logos_april_psf_1114c0f2cb-3d714801-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_april_psf_1114c0f2cb-3d714801-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_april_psf_1114c0f2cb-3d714801-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h4 id="logos-cells"><strong>Logos Cells</strong></h4><p>Logos Cells are self-organising local groups that meet regularly to address community issues with a hands-on approach. We’re currently planning a prototype Cell to determine optimum organisational structures. The learnings from this initiative will be used to provide documentation on best practices, which should guide the Logos community when establishing their own Cells to tackle local problems in a bottom-up, grassroots fashion.&nbsp;</p><p>By building relationships within themselves and their communities around our key values and issues, Cells will form an on-the-ground power base for the Logos network state. Stay tuned for more information on Logos Cells and how you can get involved.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="learning">Learning</h3><h4 id="april-s-x-spaces"><strong>April’s X Spaces</strong></h4><p>Our regular X Spaces give the Logos community a forum to discuss ideas surrounding the post-state-governance movement and the technologies needed to bring them to life. The conversations feature guests from allied network state projects and run every Thursday.</p><p>Here's what we covered in April:</p><ul><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/i/spaces/1nAJEgnvPEkKL">Access, Exit, and Exile: Engaging with the Cyberstate</a> (with Veronica Schenk of Zuzalu)</li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/i/spaces/1BRJjmjlVvdGw">Economics and Politics of the Cyberstate</a> (with Michel Bauwens of P2P Foundation)</li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/i/spaces/1yNGaLemEVDKj">Meaning of the Network State</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/i/spaces/1mrxmPWLNDVJy">Are Nation States Obsolete?</a> (with author and philosopher Peter Ludlow)</li></ul><p>Logos co-founder and co-author of&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i>, Jarrad Hope, also joined Web3 Privacy Now on&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/i/spaces/1BdxYqNDqMYxX">a recent X Space</a> to discuss the book’s ideas.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/farewell-to-westphalia">Learn more about&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia&nbsp;</i>and sign up for publishing updates</a>.</p><h3 id="operators">Operators</h3><p>Waku contributors are working hard to integrate the privacy-preserving communications protocols into&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://dashboard.logos.co/opchan/">OpChan</a>, showcasing Logos technologies and bringing the platform more in line with our principles of censorship resistance and community self-sovereignty.
