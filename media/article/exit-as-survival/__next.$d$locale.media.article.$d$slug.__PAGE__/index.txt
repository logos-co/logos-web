I let the facts land as they are.</p><p>I made myself a bridge between lived experience and technical implementation. Between something human and something buildable.</p><p>Because I understand what it means to need to leave and not have a safe way out. I understand how systems fail, quietly and repeatedly, at the exact moment they're needed most.</p><p>This isn't about the number of pull requests from unique contributors. It isn't about which chain has the most validators.
