17:T6675,<h2 id="field-station-rajasthan-a-living-experiment-in-parallel-society-building">Field Station / Rajasthan: A living experiment in parallel society building</h2><p>In October, Logos and Zu-Grama brought 30 builders, researchers, activists, and community organisers together for Field Station / Rajasthan, an eight-day experiment in parallel organising at Dhun, a regenerative farm in Jaipur district. Through living, working, and building together, residents explored what a parallel society could look like in practice: communities with the tools and shared responsibility to organise, cooperate, and solve problems for themselves.</p><p><img alt="fs-raj-logos1.jpg" src="/media-images/fs_raj_logos1_ec05f8e290-93781b23-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/fs_raj_logos1_ec05f8e290-93781b23-750.webp 750w, /media-images/fs_raj_logos1_ec05f8e290-93781b23-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="4000" height="3000" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>The residency connected that mission to local challenges, producing 15 projects, using Logos Basecamp, four winning prototypes and eight submissions for the Zu-Grama d/acc track. From the winners,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://offlinezcash.com/">Offline Zcash</a> explores private payments without an internet connection;&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/merkle-groot/bosphorus">Bosphorus</a> enables private lending inside Logos Basecamp;&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://pukaar-logos.vercel.app/">Pukaar</a> lets residents report issues and verify completed repairs; and&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/abhinavnowinew/dhun-commons">Dhun Commons</a> provides a shared treasury and coordination tool for farmers and land stewards.</p><p><img alt="rajasthan_stars.jpg" src="/media-images/rajasthan_stars_853fdfde45-a128fa79-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/rajasthan_stars_853fdfde45-a128fa79-750.webp 750w, /media-images/rajasthan_stars_853fdfde45-a128fa79-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="4032" height="2816" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Each winning team will receive milestone-based grant funding and mentoring from Logos and Zu-Grama to further develop its prototype. Connecting the projects with Logos Circles will give local communities opportunities to help shape and use the tools, extending the experiment beyond the residency. The wider cohort will continue working together through reviews, monthly meet-ups, and shared follow-up builds.</p><p><i><img alt="rajasthan.jpg" src="/media-images/rajasthan_24ffb16a85-834d6fbc-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/rajasthan_24ffb16a85-834d6fbc-750.webp 750w, /media-images/rajasthan_24ffb16a85-834d6fbc-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="4000" height="2252" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></i></p><p><i>You can learn more about Field Station / Rajasthan and the projects that emerged from it in upcoming articles and case studies on Logos Blog.</i></p><h2 id="nick-merrill-from-fighting-surveillance-to-building-alternatives">Nick Merrill: From Fighting Surveillance to Building Alternatives</h2><p>Last month, we spoke with Nick Merrill, the internet service provider who challenged an FBI demand for customer records, resulting in a gag order that prevented him from discussing it. In the interview, Nick recounts his decade-long legal battle and how it shaped his work building tools to protect people’s privacy.</p><p>Surveillance agencies will keep seeking access to our data. Nick’s experience shows why privacy must be built into the infrastructure we use, limiting what can be collected, exposed, or handed over in the first place.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/media/article/nic-merrill-interview"><i>Read more about Nick’s story.</i></a></p><div class="media-detail-video-frame"><div class="media-detail-video-aspect"><iframe title="https://youtu.be/yxdZWtd3epE" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/yxdZWtd3epE" class="media-detail-video" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div></div><figure class="media"></figure><h2 id="experience-corruption-through-nedu-s-choices">Experience corruption through Nedu’s choices&nbsp;&nbsp;</h2><p>“Life’s Choices” continues with Nedu, a young non-profit worker determined to improve his community in Lagos, Nigeria.
