10:T3bf1,<h2 id="movement-building">Movement building</h2><p>Technology is nothing without humans. Developing, maintaining, deploying, and building upon technological solutions capable of supporting diverse governance structures for communities with often wildly differing values requires an active effort to preserve the infrastructure’s political neutrality.</p><p>Logos’ near-term mission is to nurture a community prepared to defend the stack’s neutrality, ensuring that the technologies we build – and the applications and institutions built upon them – are optimised to support unprecedented governance experimentation. We round up our recent community-building efforts below.</p><h3 id="operators">Operators</h3><p><img alt="Logos-march-25-epoch2.png" src="/media-images/Logos_march_25_epoch2_590529a4b5-be188d51-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/Logos_march_25_epoch2_590529a4b5-be188d51-750.webp 750w, /media-images/Logos_march_25_epoch2_590529a4b5-be188d51-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>March saw the start of Epoch 2 for Logos Operators. If&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://gitbook.logos.co/operator-dashboard/about-the-operator-dashboard/epoch-1-the-birth-of-a-cybernation">Epoch 1</a> birthed our cybernation,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://gitbook.logos.co/operator-dashboard/about-the-operator-dashboard/epoch-2-seeding-self-sovereign-communities">Epoch 2</a> marks the transition from foundation to expansion. Like Epoch 1, Epoch 2 will include an inscription event, giving those aligned with the Logos mission another opportunity to demonstrate their commitment to our collective cause.</p><p>Details about the upcoming inscription event will be revealed soon. Follow&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/exit_operator">OP1 on X</a> to stay up to date with these and other Logos Operator initiatives, or&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.com/invite/logosnetwork">join the Logos Discord</a>.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="opchan">OpChan</h3><p>Coinciding with Epoch 2 is the beta release of OpChan, which is rapidly emerging as the messageboard for the network state movement. As the name suggests, OpChan has similarities with the iconic imageboard 4Chan. However, OpChan will progressively leverage Logos technologies to provide more robust community sovereignty while serving as a testbed for governance experiments involving Codex and Waku. OpChan’s upcoming beta release will fix many of the bugs identified by Operators testing the alpha release.&nbsp;</p><p>Since its February alpha launch, OpChan has seen network state pioneers establish&nbsp;more than 40&nbsp;Cells around a variety of topics, initiatives, and interests. Logos Cells are self-sovereign hubs for education, experimentation, and community organisation, and anyone holding a Logos Operator ordinal can establish new Cells. Learn more about&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://gitbook.logos.co/experiments/opchan/logos-cells">Logos Cells here</a>.</p><p>Participating on OpChan – either by creating Cells or contributing to the discussions within them – provides another way for both ordinal holders and non-holders to earn XP, which will be used in future Logos initiatives.</p><p>To get started on OpChan, visit the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://dashboard.logos.co/">Logos dashboard</a>, connect your Bitcoin wallet, toggle “Degen mode” at the bottom of the page, and then&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://dashboard.logos.co/opchan/">click OpChan</a> on the site navigation bar.</p><h3 id="events">Events</h3><p><img alt="Logos-march-25-PSF.png" src="/media-images/Logos_march_25_PSF_1de0a3d36b-1b30625f-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/Logos_march_25_PSF_1de0a3d36b-1b30625f-750.webp 750w, /media-images/Logos_march_25_PSF_1de0a3d36b-1b30625f-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://psc.logos.co/">Last year’s Parallel Society Congress</a> was a roaring success and planning is underway for the next edition. As community is so central to the Logos mission, we’re doubling down on the cultural aspects of the event, inviting coalition partners to take a more active role in its curation alongside Logos. This shift in focus has led to a rebrand – from PSC to Parallel Society Festival.&nbsp;</p><p>We’ll be co-hosting activations and events with the PSF coalition throughout 2025, with PSF itself tentatively scheduled for early 2026. We’ll be sharing more details with the community very soon.</p><h3 id="learning">Learning</h3><p>As the hype around the concept of network states continues to grow, Logos co-founder Jarrad Hope took the opportunity to define network states for the cryptocurrency price site CoinMarketCap in March. You can read Jarrad’s definition of a network state&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/glossary/network-states">here</a>.</p><p>Work continues to prepare Jarrad’s forthcoming book,&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i>,<i>&nbsp;</i>for publication later this year. Co-authored by friend of Logos and author and philosopher Peter Ludlow, the book provides a blueprint for leveraging blockchain and related technologies to establish parallel governance structures.</p><p><img alt="LPE-March_2025.jpg" src="/media-images/LPE_March_2025_0def67885d-440fd360-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/LPE_March_2025_0def67885d-440fd360-750.webp 750w, /media-images/LPE_March_2025_0def67885d-440fd360-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Although still unpublished, prerelease copies circulated to personal friends of the authors are already making an impression on their readers – check&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i>’s&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/Crypto_Sovereignty_and_Post-Nation-State_Governance">entry on the P2P Foundation’s Wiki</a>, for example.</p><p>To receive exclusive previews and publishing updates about&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalila</i>, sign up to&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/farewell-to-westphalia">the book’s mailing list now</a>.</p><p>Logos Press Engine is not just an educational resource; it’s also a platform to bring the work of thought leaders from outside the organisation to a wider audience. We were honoured to republish Pavol Lupták’s “The Crisis of Homogeneous Authoritative Systems and the Future of Modern Tribes”, originally published in issue two of&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://agorist.xyz/"><i>Agorism in the 21st Century</i></a>.</p><p>The article provides a sobering look at legacy systems and how we transition out of them into more freedom-oriented spaces and virtual communities.&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/crisis-homogeneous-authoritative-systems-future-modern-tribes">You can read it here</a>.</p><p>Finally, we have resumed regular X Spaces to provide an open forum for conversations on topics surrounding the parallel society movement. The spaces will often feature guests, including pioneers working at the frontier of network states, as well as Logos core contributors.&nbsp;</p><p>We hosted spaces on&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/exit_operator/status/1896954916071170547">4 March</a>,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network/status/1899508698247999754">11 March</a>,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network/status/1900241233290944897">13 March</a>,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network/status/1905305431565541539">27 March</a>, and&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network/status/1902755156690612564">20 March</a> – the latter with Daniel Thompson of Noma Collective, who discussed the overlap between digital nomadism and network states.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">Follow Logos on X</a> and join us for the next Logos Space.</p><h2 id="technology-development">Technology development</h2><h3 id="waku">Waku</h3><p><img alt="Logos-march-25-waku.png" src="/media-images/Logos_march_25_waku_a0f7e8ea64-aeff796f-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/Logos_march_25_waku_a0f7e8ea64-aeff796f-750.webp 750w, /media-images/Logos_march_25_waku_a0f7e8ea64-aeff796f-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1280" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>The development highlight from the Waku team in March was an internal hackathon that spawned several interesting project demos:</p><ul><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/threeproto/waku-sign-extension/tree/ws">Waku sign browser extension proof of concept</a>.&nbsp;</li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.whisperbox.app/">WhisperBox MVP</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://weboko.github.io/waku-phone/">Waku Phone</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/waku-org/lab.waku.org/pull/117">Passkey for RLN</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/adklempner/tauri-waku/tree/hackathon">Waku Remote</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/gabrielmer/waku-name-service">WNS</a> (Waku Name Service)</li></ul><p>Also in March, the team released a new version of js-waku – the JavaScript implementation of the privacy-preserving P2P communications protocols.
