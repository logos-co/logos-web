3f:T728c,<h2 id="logos-testnet-v0-3-release">Logos Testnet v0.3 release</h2><p>In September, Logos Testnet v0.3 was released, with improvements reaching almost every part of the stack, from how new participants join the network to how files and messages move between peers.</p><p>With EmPoWering live, participants can now get testnet tokens by performing computational work rather than requesting them centrally from a faucet. Logos Storage now allows downloads over Mix, and&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/logos-co/logos-basecamp/releases/tag/0.3.1">Basecamp</a> added Windows support while also getting early versions of Ethereum and Monero wallets.</p><p>Other additions in Testnet v0.3 include Logos SQL, a distributed SQL database run over a blockchain channel, and Channel Notes, which allows tokens bridged on the base layer to be staked by a zone’s sequencers.</p><p><a href="/media/article/logos-testnet-v03-live#proof-of-work-participation-with-empowering"><i>Learn more about Testnet v0.3</i></a><i> /&nbsp;</i><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/logos-co/logos-basecamp/releases/tag/0.3.1"><i>Run Basecamp and explore Testnet v0.3</i></a><i> /&nbsp;</i><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://docs.logos.co/run-a-node"><i>Read the docs</i></a></p><h3 id="local-first-full-stack-privacy">Local-first full-stack privacy</h3><p>The Logos stack is engineered for truly private finance (PriFi). PriFi is a system design philosophy that aims to secure the entire transaction lifecycle – the steps involved in making any deal, transfer, or exchange that occur before and after settlement. Blockchain secures settlement uniquely well, but absolute transparency onchain introduces leaks at other links in the cycle.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:3840/2160;" alt="Transaction_Supply_Chain_4.png" src="/media-images/Transaction_Supply_Chain_4_29113678d1-f1167298-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/Transaction_Supply_Chain_4_29113678d1-f1167298-750.webp 750w, /media-images/Transaction_Supply_Chain_4_29113678d1-f1167298-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="3840" height="2160" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><p>Capital risks and the costs of protecting against them pose a barrier to entry for many market participants. PriFi infrastructure is engineered to disclose information only to necessary parties and shield by default. It runs P2P on users’ computers to minimise reliance on vulnerable proprietary hardware, while employing mechanisms to provide maximum privacy across the stack. By reducing costs and risks across the lifecycle, PriFi provides greater confidentiality and thus security than was previously attainable only by a wealthy few.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/prifi"><i>Learn more about PriFi</i></a><i> /&nbsp;</i><a href="/media/article/pri-fi-securing-transaction-supply-chain"><i>Explore why PriFi matters&nbsp;</i></a><i>&nbsp;&nbsp;</i></p><h2 id="local-area-network-launches">Local Area Network launches</h2><p>September saw the relaunch of the ever-popular Logos zine.
