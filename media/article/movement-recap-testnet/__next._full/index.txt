42:T600d,<p><a href="/media/article/testnet-v02-live">Logos Testnet v0.2 went live at the end of June</a>. The release deploys and hardens the core protocols of the stack – Messaging, Storage, and Blockchain – while introducing foundational privacy features.&nbsp;</p><p>Logos Testnet v0.1 provided valuable insights into the network’s stability and uncovered bugs to iron out since its release at Parallel Society in March. During that time, the movement has been expanding and taking action to promote Logos tech and vision.&nbsp;</p><p>Read on as we recap initiatives undertaken by those developing on and organising around the Logos tech stack during the Testnet v0.1 period. The event served as a way station for those inspired by our mission to rebuild civil society and coincided with the release of Logos Testnet v0.1.&nbsp;</p><p>Around 2,400 activists, developers, artists, and optimists converged in Lisbon to explore the frontier we’re building. Supporting the [un]conference programme over the two dayer was a pack of pioneering organisations also at the forefront of the parallel governance movement, including Nym, Internet Archive, Sealand, Tor, DarkFi, and many other aligned groups.</p><p><img alt="logos-recap-testnet-v1-os-logos.png" src="/media-images/logos_recap_testnet_v1_os_logos_bd28e143c5-618a3e89-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_recap_testnet_v1_os_logos_bd28e143c5-618a3e89-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_recap_testnet_v1_os_logos_bd28e143c5-618a3e89-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1448" height="1086" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Opening the diverse Day 1 schedule of practical and philosophical sessions, the movement’s cofounder Jarrad Hope spoke about why Logos is now more important than ever, before Logos engineers demoed Testnet v0.1 and Logos modules. They’re the beating heart of the movement. <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://circles.logos.co/">Learn more</a>.</p><p>Since Testnet v0.1 release:</p><ul><li>Total Circles: 88</li><li>Host Cities: 42 (in 20 countries)</li><li>New Circles: 21</li><li>Total attendees: 840</li><li>Circles running a Logos node: 6</li></ul><p><img alt="circles-map-logos-recap.jpg" src="/media-images/circles_map_logos_recap_d91de70432-85eae82f-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/circles_map_logos_recap_d91de70432-85eae82f-750.webp 750w, /media-images/circles_map_logos_recap_d91de70432-85eae82f-1020.webp 1020w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1020" height="423" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Since the testnet launch, Logos Circles have been making good progress on the winnable issues they’ve identified.</p><p><strong>Logos Circle / Abeokuta</strong> is advancing two winnable issues focused on transparent community funding and women’s economic empowerment. The Circle has launched a crypto donations page on FundBrave, enabling direct and cross-blockchain contributions with transparent, community-controlled fund management. It is also fundraising to support at least 20 women entrepreneurs in accessing online courses on digital skills, business development, and technical fundamentals; this second initiative is currently in progress.&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/upskilling-support-abeokuta-enugu">Learn more</a>.</p><p><strong>Logos Circle / Accra</strong> addressed Ghana’s digital skills gap through a practical literacy programme covering email setup, research, document creation, productivity tools, and online safety. The Circle successfully delivered monthly hands-on sessions in April, May, and June and is now expanding the curriculum with peer learning groups and repeatable exercises to reinforce learning between sessions.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://circles.logos.co/readme/about-logos-circles/winnable-issues-from-circles#accra-ghana">Learn more</a>.</p><p><strong>Logos Circle / Awka&nbsp;</strong>is improving job readiness among local tech talent through practical CV training, expert guidance, and structured mentorship. After attracting more than 70 participants to its CV and job application session, the Circle launched a personalised CV review initiative and an impact-tracking system to measure interviews and job placements. It has also completed its first school outreach at a local school, providing learning materials, educator welfare packages, and student development talks, while work is ongoing to establish a sustainable funding and logistics model for future education initiatives.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://circles.logos.co/readme/about-logos-circles/winnable-issues-from-circles#awka-nigeria">Learn more</a>.</p><h3 id="documenting-circles">Documenting Circles</h3><p>To highlight the work of Circles worldwide, we’ve produced a video series focused on three of the longest-running Circles: Lisbon, LA, and London.</p><p><strong>Logos Circle / Lisbon</strong> has responded to the city’s housing crisis by bringing people together to share stories, support one another, and develop collective solutions. Their efforts have already helped secure a community centre where neighbours can organise and connect.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://forum.logos.co/t/lisbon-circle-july/1751">Learn more</a>.</p><div class="media-detail-video-frame"><div class="media-detail-video-aspect"><iframe title="https://youtu.be/c8N9Nng5fz0?si=gn3pdz8HtHnkJMns" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/c8N9Nng5fz0" class="media-detail-video" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div></div><figure class="media"></figure><p><strong>Logos Circle / Los Angeles</strong> is developing a private whistleblowing app for musicians, artists, actors, and other entertainment workers to report exploitation without putting themselves or their careers at risk. By creating safer ways to speak up and support one another, the Circle aims to challenge the silence that has long shaped parts of the entertainment industry.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://forum.logos.co/t/logos-circle-los-angeles/485/31">Learn more</a>.</p><div class="media-detail-video-frame"><div class="media-detail-video-aspect"><iframe title="https://youtu.be/33BKS911V6U?si=A9VnP1yUZtrVaTd9" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/33BKS911V6U" class="media-detail-video" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div></div><figure class="media"></figure><p><strong>Logos Circle / London</strong> is contributing to policy discussions on the UK’s £114 billion annual interest payments, raising awareness on how public money is allocated and what it means for real people. The Circle is also focused on digital IDs and chat control, and the threat to civil liberties they pose. More below&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/ukdebt">and here</a>.</p><div class="media-detail-video-frame"><div class="media-detail-video-aspect"><iframe title="https://youtu.be/h0T6uFnsOo4?si=tbqDGksHI90EOcaO" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/h0T6uFnsOo4" class="media-detail-video" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div></div><figure class="media"></figure><h3 id="tooling-the-network">Tooling the network</h3><p>The expansion of Logos Circles worldwide creates new opportunities to deploy Logos technologies to solve real-world problems. Although the stack is still in testnet phase, Circle members have been exploring potential applications of the tech, as well as helping others to run their own infrastructure. Circles in Lisbon, New Delhi, Brno, Los Angeles, Florianópolis, and Rome are already running a Logos node.</p><p>Shayan from&nbsp;<strong>Logos Circle / Lisbon&nbsp;</strong>not only helped to spin up a node for the Circle but also gave a live workshop on how to do it. Earlier,&nbsp;<strong>Logos Circle / Mexico City</strong> <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.youtube.com/live/UTI1wI9ErmQ">posted a online tutorial in Spanish</a> on how to run a node.</p><p><img alt="node-workshop-lisbon-circle-logos.png" src="/media-images/node_workshop_lisbon_circle_logos_7f74d32430-5aedfa1f-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/node_workshop_lisbon_circle_logos_7f74d32430-5aedfa1f-750.webp 750w, /media-images/node_workshop_lisbon_circle_logos_7f74d32430-5aedfa1f-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1445" height="1088" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p><strong>Logos Circle / Enugu</strong> is exploring how the Logos stack can support a community-owned mental health support system for University of Nigeria Nsukka students, combining anonymous first-contact support through Logos Messaging, a verified resource library on Logos Storage, and a community-governed therapy subsidy fund on Logos Blockchain. The Circle has drafted partnership proposals with the UNN Department of Psychology and Bespoke Therapeutic Consults to provide ethical oversight, peer-support training, clinical supervision, and professional referrals.&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/upskilling-support-abeokuta-enugu">Learn more</a>.&nbsp;</p><p><strong>Logos Circle / Ilorin</strong> is developing Wahala, an emergency response platform that centralises verified emergency contacts, sends SOS alerts, and shares real-time incident updates. The proposed solution would use smart contracts and multi-signature approvals to lock funds and ensure they are released only for their stated purpose, making payments visible and accountable. The initiative is still at an early stage, with the Circle now seeking local institutions for pilot programmes and preparing a proof-of-concept smart contract on testnet.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://circles.logos.co/readme/about-logos-circles/winnable-issues-from-circles#kampala-uganda">Learn more</a>.</p><h3 id="pop-up-circles">Pop-Up Circles</h3><p>Pop-Up Circles form to channel the energy of existing gatherings into collective actions. Since the testnet release, Pop-Up Circles have appeared in Liberland, Berlin, and at DWeb Camp.</p><p><strong>Pop-Up Circle / Liberland&nbsp;</strong>formed during the micronation’s 11th anniversary celebrations in April. After hearing about a neglected road between Liberland and neighbouring Serbia, the Logos community members at the event gathered a group and got to work.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>In a few hours, they removed more than 35 bags of rubbish from a roadside and riverbank. The one-day cleanup attracted spontaneous help from locals, earned media coverage, and got a shoutout from the President of Liberland.&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/circles-liberland-cleanup">Learn more</a>.</p><p><img alt="rubbish_pile_1-liberland-circles-cleanup.jpg" src="/media-images/rubbish_pile_1_liberland_circles_cleanup_2924c5d306-19c46130-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/rubbish_pile_1_liberland_circles_cleanup_2924c5d306-19c46130-750.webp 750w, /media-images/rubbish_pile_1_liberland_circles_cleanup_2924c5d306-19c46130-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2000" height="1050" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p><strong>Pop-Up Circle / Berlin&nbsp;</strong>formed during Berlin Blockchain Week in June. The ad hoc group used a quadratic voting platform to hone in on an issue and get to work quickly. They landed on a rubbish cleanup in a local park after which they shared a meal. Working closely to solve real issues sparked conversations that wouldn’t happen in a conference room and proved to be a valuable network-building experience. Again, locals seeing the action were thankful for the Circle’s efforts.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://forum.logos.co/t/field-note-build-the-parallel-action-not-permission-berlin-june-2026/1737">Learn more</a>.&nbsp;</p><p><img alt="berlin-cleanup-june-update.png" src="/media-images/berlin_cleanup_june_update_09b774a27b-aec99087-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/berlin_cleanup_june_update_09b774a27b-aec99087-750.webp 750w, /media-images/berlin_cleanup_june_update_09b774a27b-aec99087-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1448" height="1086" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p><strong>Pop-Up Circle / DWeb Camp</strong></p><p>A hands-on Pop-Up Circles at DWeb Camp session gave participants the chance to identify a concrete, winnable issue and coordinate action around it. In 2026, the government is expected to spend £114 billion on interest alone. These funds do not build new homes, hospitals, schools, roads, or reduce the debt burden; they are simply the cost of past decisions.</p><p>Logos infrastructure is designed to support monetary and financial primitives to underpin a growing parallel economy, reducing dependence on legacy systems. Raising awareness that alternatives are possible and viable is crucial for their eventual adoption.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/ukdebt">Learn more</a> and join&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.com/channels/973324189794697286/1435273609940832428"><strong>Logos Circle / London&nbsp;</strong>to support the campaign</a>.</p><p><img alt="uk-debt-interest-logos-june-update.png" src="/media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-750.webp 750w, /media-images/uk_debt_interest_logos_june_update_be55c6e2ce-71a2ad6e-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1200" height="630" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h2 id="tools-not-permission">Tools, Not Permission</h2><h3 id="logos-lan">Logos / LAN</h3><p>Logos / LANs are tech-focused gatherings to workshop, demo, and build on the Logos stack.</p><p><strong>Logos LAN / Berlin&nbsp;</strong>took place over two sessions alongside Berlin Blockchain Week in June.
