f:T5fd3,<h2 id="museum-of-civil-liberties-opens">Museum of Civil Liberties opens</h2><p>In August, Past.Present.Future., a new initiative exploring the systems that shape our freedoms and the forces that continue to erode them, went live. It begins with The Past and a trip to the Museum of Civil Liberties, an online museum tracing pivotal moments when our agency over money, communication, and political expression was weakened. The museum is a reminder of why privacy-preserving, censorship-resistant technologies are at the heart of Logos.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:1920/1080;" alt="museum-civ-libs-exhibit-aug-logos-update.jpeg" src="/media-images/museum_civ_libs_exhibit_aug_logos_update_cd62fa9ef6-70903a08-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/museum_civ_libs_exhibit_aug_logos_update_cd62fa9ef6-70903a08-750.webp 750w, /media-images/museum_civ_libs_exhibit_aug_logos_update_cd62fa9ef6-70903a08-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><p>Enter the museum to explore the events that brought us here, from the end of the gold standard and the expansion of post-9/11 surveillance to internet shutdowns and the weaponisation of personal data.&nbsp;</p><p>The Past is only the beginning: The Present and The Future are coming soon, extending the story of our eroding freedoms and where we’re heading if we don’t change course.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/past-present-future/museum-of-civil-liberties/">Enter the Museum of Civil Liberties.</a></p><p><img alt="museum-civil-liberties-aug-logos-update.jpg" src="/media-images/museum_civil_liberties_aug_logos_update_3b829f6984-b047a889-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/museum_civil_liberties_aug_logos_update_3b829f6984-b047a889-750.webp 750w, /media-images/museum_civil_liberties_aug_logos_update_3b829f6984-b047a889-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h2 id="raising-chat-control-awareness">Raising chat control awareness&nbsp;</h2><p>Last month, Logos launched a new initiative examining the EU and UK’s latest efforts to regulate online safety through private-message scanning and age verification. Both are presented as targeted measures to protect children, but in practice they require much broader systems for inspecting communications or verifying the identities of ordinary users.</p><p><img alt="chat-control-logos-update-aug-26.png" src="/media-images/chat_control_logos_update_aug_26_4ece373bdf-6ad929e5-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/chat_control_logos_update_aug_26_4ece373bdf-6ad929e5-750.webp 750w, /media-images/chat_control_logos_update_aug_26_4ece373bdf-6ad929e5-1080.webp 1080w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1080" height="1350" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>The initiative presents peer-reviewed research, large-scale empirical studies, and evidence from leading cryptographers and security researchers to show why these measures will fail at their stated goals while imposing significant threats to privacy and freedom. This is a core reason Logos exists: to build privacy-preserving, censorship-resistant technologies that uphold freedoms structurally rather than waiting for policy promises that rarely result in lasting change.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/chatcontrol">Check out the chat control initiative homepage.</a></p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.com/channels/973324189794697286/1435273609940832428">If you’re local, join Logos Circle / London to support.</a></p><h2 id="muster-on-logos-showing-privacy-leaks-across-the-transaction-lifecycle">Muster on Logos: Showing privacy leaks across the transaction lifecycle</h2><p>This month, Corey shared Muster, a sample application built on Logos to explore what privacy looks like across the full lifecycle of a transaction, not just at the point where value moves onchain. The demo brings encrypted messaging, coordination, negotiation, and private settlement into a single workflow, while also surfacing where information is exposed along the way.</p><p><img alt="muster-app-logos-update-aug-26.png" src="/media-images/muster_app_logos_update_aug_26_71f9b3f276-b23fe081-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/muster_app_logos_update_aug_26_71f9b3f276-b23fe081-750.webp 750w, /media-images/muster_app_logos_update_aug_26_71f9b3f276-b23fe081-1046.webp 1046w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1046" height="828" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Muster provides a useful demonstration of the PriFi thesis: protecting a transaction at settlement is not enough if intent, counterparties, terms, or identity leak through the tools used before and after it. By keeping more of that activity within the same privacy-oriented infrastructure, Logos can reduce dependence on fragmented, centrally operated services, and give users greater visibility and control over what they reveal.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://forum.research.logos.co/t/the-transaction-supply-chain-part-1-coming-together-with-an-intended-action/723">Learn more</a> and&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/corpetty/muster/tree/main">experiment with Muster</a>.</p><h2 id="parallel-dispatch-launches">Parallel Dispatch launches</h2><p><img alt="parallel-dispatch-aug-logos-update.jpg" src="/media-images/parallel_dispatch_aug_logos_update_e952671221-75c60520-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/parallel_dispatch_aug_logos_update_e952671221-75c60520-750.webp 750w, /media-images/parallel_dispatch_aug_logos_update_e952671221-75c60520-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Parallel Dispatch is a new biweekly newsletter that highlights developments threatening our freedoms and the tech and people defending them. Expect a curated selection of topical stories covering legal updates, activist movements, and resistance technologies from around the world. Sign up at the bottom of the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">Logos homepage</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>Parallel Dispatch also features bite-sized interviews with pioneers building, theorising, or taking action to support the parallel society movement. The first was with Ann Brody,&nbsp;an interdisciplinary researcher and digital rights advocate whose work explores how decentralised technologies shape governance and collective organising.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:1440/800;" alt="ann-brody-parallel-dispatch-aug-logos-update.jpg" src="/media-images/ann_brody_parallel_dispatch_aug_logos_update_fef988de94-df7a15f5-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/ann_brody_parallel_dispatch_aug_logos_update_fef988de94-df7a15f5-750.webp 750w, /media-images/ann_brody_parallel_dispatch_aug_logos_update_fef988de94-df7a15f5-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1440" height="800" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><h2 id="irl-upcoming">IRL / Upcoming</h2><p>Over the coming months, Logos is exploring plans for a linked series of residencies, technical gatherings, and cultural activations focused on privacy, coordination, and developing real world solutions on sovereign infrastructure.</p><p>Potential activities include hands-on experimentation with the Logos stack, community-led organising, and collaborations with artists and independent networks. Stay tuned for updates. More details will be shared as plans develop,</p><h2 id="irl-august">IRL / August</h2><h3 id="zanzalu">Zanzalu</h3><p>Over three weeks at Zanzalu, the Logos community put both its technology and its approach to grassroots organising into practice. Participants explored Basecamp, learned how to get Logos nodes online, joined permaculture training, and connected with local projects to share skills and learn from existing community initiatives.</p><p><img alt="zanzalu-circle-logos-update-aug-26.png" src="/media-images/zanzalu_circle_logos_update_aug_26_23fee93dfc-dba831ea-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/zanzalu_circle_logos_update_aug_26_23fee93dfc-dba831ea-750.webp 750w, /media-images/zanzalu_circle_logos_update_aug_26_23fee93dfc-dba831ea-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1448" height="1086" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>A highlight was returning to Straight Training Center, a community-run school supporting more than 120 students in Zanzibar. Last year,&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/logos-circles-winnable-issues">Logos Circle / Zanzibar helped raise funds</a> for 10 Raspberry Pis to equip its computer room. This year, the team saw those computers in use, delivered children’s books collected through Logos Circle / Lisbon, ran English reading and conversation classes, and took part in music and cultural exchange sessions with students.</p><p><img alt="zanzalu2-circle-update-aug-26.png" src="/media-images/zanzalu2_circle_update_aug_26_8a0a36dbf3-5c7e0cad-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/zanzalu2_circle_update_aug_26_8a0a36dbf3-5c7e0cad-750.webp 750w, /media-images/zanzalu2_circle_update_aug_26_8a0a36dbf3-5c7e0cad-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1448" height="1086" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>The visit also helped lay the groundwork for what comes next.
