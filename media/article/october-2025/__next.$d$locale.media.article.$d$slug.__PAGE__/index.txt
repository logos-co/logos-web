11:T6af3,<h1 id="cultivating-community">Cultivating community</h1><p>Logos is a movement dedicated to redefining the way people connect and collaborate. Bringing this vision to reality depends on the drive and imagination of a passionate community.&nbsp;</p><p>If&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">our principles</a> resonate with you, we invite you to share your skills and insights. Everyone who builds with us receives recognition and support.</p><p>The Logos Contribute portal makes it easy to participate, whether by writing code to advance the Logos technology stack; building on it; or supporting the movement with creative, non-technical ideas.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://contribute.logos.co/en/">Submit your proposed contribution through the portal</a>, and we’ll help you get started.</p><h2 id="campaigns">Campaigns</h2><h3 id="privacy-in-practice">Privacy in Practice</h3><p>Last month, we sponsored two tracks as part of RealFi Hack, a virtual hackathon encouraging the building of tools that people actually need today. The Logos-sponsored Resilient Activist Technology and Logos x Tor Privacy infrastructure tracks produced some very promising submissions, resulting in two winners per category. Each winning team received $2,000 in bounties and a <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://keycard.tech/">Keycard</a> for each member.</p><p><img in-depth details about each of the winning entries.&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/realfi-hack">Read it now</a>.</p><p>We’ll also be giving a technical overview of winning projects on X each week.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network/status/1983171690604761505">Watch the Ash technical overview now</a> and follow&nbsp;<a target="_blank" Aires in November,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/campaigns/devconnect2025">learn more about each initiative and how to get involved here</a>.</p><h2 id="learning">Learning</h2><h3 id="farewell-to-westphalia"><i>Farewell to Westphalia</i></h3><p>In September, Logos Press Engine released its debut book,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/farewell-to-westphalia"><i>Farewell to Westphalia</i></a>, and our promotional efforts continued into October. Authored by Logos cofounder Jarrad Hope and Peter Ludlow (author of&nbsp;<i>Crypto Anarchy, Cyberstates, and Pirate Utopias</i>), it examines how blockchain technology can be applied across different layers of collaboration. Its central thesis is that reinventing our systems of governance can reduce corruption and foster continuous experimentation and optimisation, leading to greater human flourishing.</p><p>The book is available now in both physical and ereader editions via major online bookstores and as a free digital download on the Logos website.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/farewell-to-westphalia/">Read it now</a>.</p><div class="media-detail-video-frame"><div class="media-detail-video-aspect"><iframe title="https://youtu.be/T0kNpazXeWU" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/T0kNpazXeWU" class="media-detail-video" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div></div><figure class="media"></figure><p>If you’ve already read&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i> and found it insightful, we’d greatly appreciate it if you could leave a short review on&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/234639991-farewell-to-westphalia">Goodreads</a> or&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.amazon.com/Farewell-Westphalia-Sovereignty-Post-Nation-State-Governance/dp/B0FQLV79ZN/">Amazon</a> to boost its visibility. The book represents a core part of the Logos vision, and sharing it widely helps invite others to engage with and support the movement.</p><p><img alt="logos-august-featured-farewell-to-westphalia.png" src="/media-images/logos_august_featured_farewell_to_westphalia_b62e0102e8-d3f34032-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_august_featured_farewell_to_westphalia_b62e0102e8-d3f34032-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_august_featured_farewell_to_westphalia_b62e0102e8-d3f34032-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1600" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h3 id="logos-press-engine">Logos Press Engine</h3><p>Logos Press Engine published an overview of the winning RealFi Hack projects in October.&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/realfi-hack">Read it now</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>We’re also working with community member Nova on a trilogy of articles that uses the Sahel coup belt as a live case study for the transition from nation-state to parallel, alternative governance. The series aims to bridge&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i>'s theoretical framework with&nbsp;<i>The Network State</i>'s implementation blueprint, illustrating one possible strategy to deploy blockchain governance in environments where legacy institutions have failed.</p><p>Want to contribute to the conversation on the future of human cooperation and governance? Submit your article proposal to Logos Press Engine via the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://contribute.logos.co/en/">Logos Contribute portal</a>.</p><h3 id="x-spaces">X Spaces&nbsp;</h3><p>Logos’ October spaces focused heavily on privacy-enhancing tech, coinciding with the ongoing Privacy in Practice campaign. We started the month with a special guest from the Internet Archive and discussed the organisation’s efforts to save the modern archive with new decentralised tooling.</p><p>The following week, Harry Halpin from Nym joined us to discuss cypherpunk philosophy, crypto anarchism, and Nym’s VPN technology. The Open Dialogue Foundation also came on to talk about how to protect privacy activists and help those who have been debanked in crypto.&nbsp;</p><p>Then, author Ben Collier, author of&nbsp;<i>Tor: From the Dark Web to the Future of Privacy</i>, treated the Logos community to a freewheeling history of Tor, its strengths, weaknesses, and criticality for preserving access to information and media on the internet. Later in the month, Mykola from Web3 Privacy Now had an in-depth conversation with us about what cypherpunk really means in today’s world, with a focus on neo-cypherpunk ideology.&nbsp;</p><p>We ended the month strong with a live broadcast Space from Edge City in Argentina. The cofounder of Edge, Timour Kosters, joined us to discuss philosophy, transhumanism, and the difference between the solarpunk and lunarpunk ethos.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">Follow Logos on X</a> to listen in and join these fascinating discussions every Thursday.&nbsp;</p><h2 id="logos-irl">Logos IRL</h2><h3 id="logos-circles">Logos Circles</h3><p>Logos Circles connect individuals both online and in community hubs across the globe. The group explored the Logos vision, the concept of Circles, and began identifying potential winnable issues.&nbsp;</li><li><strong>Doha</strong> (16 Oct): The first Circle in Doha brought 35 participants to Qatar Science and Technology Park to explore decentralisation, privacy, and digital trust.
