12:T63fb,<h2 id="logos-testnet-v0-1-now-live">Logos Testnet v0.1 now live</h2><p>March saw the first iteration of the Logos Testnet, v0.1, go live. The testnet brings together the full stack of protocols into a single, working system. Consensus, execution, networking, storage, and messaging are now integrated into a unified, modular architecture, forming the early foundations for private-by-default socio-economic infrastructure. For developers, it opens a new frontier: a live environment to run nodes, experiment with modules, and explore how these systems behave under real network conditions.</p><p>This initial testnet focuses on validating the foundations: stress testing integrations, exposing APIs, and proving that the stack functions end-to-end.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="/media/article/logos-testnet-v01">Learn more about Logos Testnet v0.1</a>.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="explore-logos-builder-hub">Explore Logos Builder Hub</h3><p>Alongside the testnet, we also launched&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://build.logos.co/">Logos Builder Hub</a> in March. If you want to explore the stack and start building, you should make Logos Builder Hub the first stop on your journey. It has everything you need in one place: documentation, a submission portal for ideas, details of our RFP process, sample apps, open Lambda Prizes, and access to support from the devs building Logos.</p><p>It's also where you can&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://github.com/logos-co/logos-basecamp">install Logos Basecamp</a>, a complete distribution that bundles the runtime, core modules (Blockchain, Messaging, and Storage), and UI into a single environment. Everything runs on hardware you control, meaning greater privacy, resilience, and data sovereignty, as well as offline functionality.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://build.logos.co/">Start hacking on the frontier via Logos Builder Hub now</a>.</p><h2 id="parallel-society-takes-over-lisbon">Parallel Society takes over Lisbon</h2><div class="media-detail-video-frame"><div class="media-detail-video-aspect"><iframe title="https://youtu.be/t_Sh_eFN9Fk" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/t_Sh_eFN9Fk" class="media-detail-video" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div></div><figure class="media"></figure><p>Parallel Society, the flagship event of the Logos calendar, brought together just under 2,500 technologists, pioneers, activists, and independent thinkers and 44 coalition partners to build, forge new bonds, and find their place in the Logos movement on 6 and 7 March.</p><p><img alt="Logos-update-parallel-society-jarrad.jpg" src="/media-images/Logos_update_parallel_society_jarrad_b008cab77c-732c1e3a-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/Logos_update_parallel_society_jarrad_b008cab77c-732c1e3a-750.webp 750w, /media-images/Logos_update_parallel_society_jarrad_b008cab77c-732c1e3a-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2160" height="1728" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Day 1 focused on the ideas and tech behind our movement. We divided the industrial-turned-creative space in Marvila, Lisbon, into five zones themed around key Logos values: Privacy, Decentralisation, Culture, Community, and Free and Open Source. Following a rousing introductory presentation by Logos cofounder Jarrad, participatory sessions spanned the venue all day.&nbsp;</p><p><img alt="logos-monthly-parallel-society-1-present.jpg" src="/media-images/logos_monthly_parallel_society_1_present_58f480fed4-f3c3a6cf-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_monthly_parallel_society_1_present_58f480fed4-f3c3a6cf-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_monthly_parallel_society_1_present_58f480fed4-f3c3a6cf-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2160" height="1728" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>From hands-on workshops like running your own Logos node and writing programs for the Execution Zone, to discussions on digital rights, governance, and decentralised dispute resolution, the programme blended practical building and inquiry into how societies coordinate. Sessions on decentralised tech, pop-up cities, and alternative economic models sat alongside community-led conversations and open workshops, charging the venue with an energy of learning, debate, and experimentation.</p><p><img alt="logos-monthly-parallel-society-1-free.jpg" src="/media-images/logos_monthly_parallel_society_1_free_d9c25964e4-4a039a7f-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_monthly_parallel_society_1_free_d9c25964e4-4a039a7f-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_monthly_parallel_society_1_free_d9c25964e4-4a039a7f-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2160" height="1728" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Day 2 of Parallel Society turned toward culture as an extension of the ideas explored on Day 1. Rooted in a DIY ethos, the day celebrated underground innovators and emerging voices, showing how culture is a layer of coordination that shapes identity, narrative, and collective meaning.</p><p><img alt="Logos-update-parallel-society-day2-1.jpg" src="/media-images/Logos_update_parallel_society_day2_1_cbcb5c6ffa-711765b3-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/Logos_update_parallel_society_day2_1_cbcb5c6ffa-711765b3-750.webp 750w, /media-images/Logos_update_parallel_society_day2_1_cbcb5c6ffa-711765b3-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2160" height="1728" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Across the Terminal and the Lab stages, a global lineup brought this vision to life. Live sets from Gilles Peterson with MC Rob Galliano, Clark, and Moses Boyd took place alongside performances and AV experiences from artists like DJ Stingray 313, Los Bitchos, Calibre, Kode9, and Violet. If Day 1 built the systems, Day 2 was all about how those systems are felt, expressed, and ultimately sustained.</p><p><img alt="PS-day2-Amir-logos-march-update.jpg" src="/media-images/PS_day2_Amir_logos_march_update_6e5c054d3b-6b210dc5-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/PS_day2_Amir_logos_march_update_6e5c054d3b-6b210dc5-750.webp 750w, /media-images/PS_day2_Amir_logos_march_update_6e5c054d3b-6b210dc5-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2160" height="1728" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>If you couldn't join us in Lisbon or just want to relive the experience, we recorded most of the Day 1 programme and Day 2 performances, and we'll put them online soon.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">Keep an eye on Logos socials to watch them as soon as they're live</a>.</p><p><img alt="PS-day2-logos-march-update.jpg" src="/media-images/PS_day2_logos_march_update_2552614132-740a82f2-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/PS_day2_logos_march_update_2552614132-740a82f2-750.webp 750w, /media-images/PS_day2_logos_march_update_2552614132-740a82f2-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2160" height="1728" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h2 id="privacy-builders-bootcamp-coming-soon">Privacy Builders Bootcamp coming soon</h2><p>We just announced the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.encodeclub.com/programmes/logos-privacy-builders-bootcamp">Logos Privacy Builders Bootcamp</a>, starting on 13 April in collaboration with Encode Club.
