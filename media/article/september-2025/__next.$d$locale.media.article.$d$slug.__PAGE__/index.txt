10:T5351,<h2 id="cultivating-community">Cultivating community</h2><p>Logos is a movement devoted to reimagining how people connect and collaborate. Bringing this vision to life relies on the energy of a committed, creative community. If&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">our values</a> speak to you, we welcome your talents and ideas. Those who build alongside us are recognised and supported.</p><p>The Logos Contribute portal simplifies the process of making technical and non-technical contributions to Logos. If you have an idea for a project that leverages our tech or have something to add to the alternative governance conversation,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://contribute.logos.co/en/">submit a proposal via the portal</a>, and we’ll help you get up and running.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="campaigns">Campaigns</h3><p><strong>Privacy in Practice</strong></p><p>Last month, we kicked off a major ongoing initiative with Funding the Commons to support the development and maintenance of the privacy-preserving communication network Tor. The Privacy in Practice campaign aims to empower individuals and communities to reclaim digital freedoms by humanising privacy technologies through real-world stories, use cases, and tool building. Crucially, the campaign encourages the development of tools and apps that real people actually need today.</p><p>As part of Privacy in Practice, Logos is sponsoring the Resilient Activist Technology and Logos x Tor Privacy Infrastructure tracks at the RealFi Virtual Hackathon (23 Sept–16 Oct). Get involved. Win bounties. Advance privacy technologies.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/campaigns/real-fi-hack">Learn more and register</a>.</p><p>The RealFi Virtual Hackathon is just one of several initiatives that we’ll be running to support the ongoing development and adoption of privacy-preserving technologies.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">Follow Logos on X</a> to stay up to date with the Privacy in Practice campaign.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:2142/1202;" alt="logos-sept-update-realfi-hack.jpeg" src="/media-images/logos_sept_update_realfi_hack_84de4e815e-c92041a8-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_sept_update_realfi_hack_84de4e815e-c92041a8-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_sept_update_realfi_hack_84de4e815e-c92041a8-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2142" height="1202" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><h3 id="learning">Learning</h3><p><i><strong>Farewell to Westphalia</strong></i></p><p>Logos Press Engine published its first book in September.&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i>, coauthored by Logos cofounder Jarrad Hope and author of&nbsp;<i>Crypto Anarchy, Cyberstates, and Pirate Utopias</i>, Peter Ludlow, explores blockchain technology’s application to all levels of human cooperation, making the case that overhauling our governance processes will disincentivise corruption while encouraging ongoing experimentation and optimisation.&nbsp;</p><p>The book is available in both physical and ereader editions via major online bookstores and as a free digital download.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/farewell-to-westphalia/">Read it now</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>We’re calling on the Logos community to support us in bringing&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i> into traditional educational spaces. If you have connections at universities, libraries, or bookshops that might be interested in carrying the book, please&nbsp;<a href="mailto:pressengine@logos.co">contact Logos Press Engine</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>If you’ve read it already and found it valuable, please take a moment to leave a review on&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/234639991-farewell-to-westphalia">Goodreads</a> or&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.amazon.com/Farewell-Westphalia-Sovereignty-Post-Nation-State-Governance/dp/B0FQLV79ZN/">Amazon</a> to help its discoverability. The book is foundational to the Logos mission, and the wider we can spread its message, the more people will join the Logos cause.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:1999/1409;" alt="logos-august-farewell-to-westphalia.png" src="/media-images/logos_august_farewell_to_westphalia_671a92960c-c3c57256-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_august_farewell_to_westphalia_671a92960c-c3c57256-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_august_farewell_to_westphalia_671a92960c-c3c57256-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1999" height="1409" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><p><strong>Logos Press Engine</strong></p><p>Logos Press Engine also published a new article about the Logos Circles initiative and the success of our first Circle in Africa in September. The Zanzalu Circle proved to be an ideal case study for identifying best practices for those organising their own Circles going forward.&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/logos-circles-winnable-issues">Read it now</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>Got insights to share on alternative governance? Propose an article for Logos Press Engine through the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://contribute.logos.co/en/">Logos Contribute portal</a> and add your voice to the conversation.</p><p><strong>X Spaces&nbsp;</strong></p><p>Our September X Spaces placed a strong emphasis on cypherpunk values, with discussions exploring both the defence of privacy and the creation of parallel governance structures. We opened the month with Michel Bauwens of the P2P Foundation, who spoke on cosmolocalism and its potential to enable communities to flourish.&nbsp;</p><p>Midway through September, we hosted Monero community members who shared their perspective on privacy, financial freedom, and activism. To close the month, Logos brought together contributors and long-standing community members for an open conversation on the transition from traditional governance structures to network-based forms of human coordination.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">Follow Logos on X</a> to listen in and join these discussions every Thursday.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="logos-irl">Logos IRL</h3><p><strong>Logos Circles</strong></p><p>Logos Circles are recurring gatherings hosted both online and in local hubs worldwide. expand the Circles initiative globally and need committed, aligned community members to lead them.&nbsp;If want to start your own Circle, introduce yourself in the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" city it passes through. Core to our strategy is supporting local creative and technological communities. In Lisbon, partners such as&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DNnnEAvoSj1/">Planting A.I.R.</a>,&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.arrozestudios.pt/rare-effect">Rare Effect,</a> and&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.instagram.com/arrozestudios/">Arroz Estúdios</a> embody this spirit.</p><p>PS will be supporting Arroz Estúdios’ Planting Artists in Residence, which works with emerging artists with low income or from underrepresented communities.
