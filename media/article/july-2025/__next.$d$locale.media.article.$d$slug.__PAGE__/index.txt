f:T42c4,<h2 id="cultivating-community">Cultivating community</h2><p>Logos is an ambitious project seeking to redefine the way humans organise. Such an undertaking can only be successful with the support of a passionate and driven community. If our&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">mission and values</a> speak to you, please bring your energy and skills to the cause. We celebrate and reward those building alongside us.</p><p>If you’re eager but struggling to find ways to help, check the #<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.com/channels/973324189794697286/1372894505396867082/1373989785714163746">Contribute</a> channel in our Discord, or you can post ideas for initiatives on the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://forum.logos.co/">Logos Forum</a>.&nbsp;</p><h3 id="logos-irl">Logos IRL</h3><p><strong>Logos Circles</strong></p><p>Logos Circles are meetups held in various hubs around the world and in cyberspace. Their aim is to expand the Logos community and identify issues that Logos technologies and grassroots collective action can address.&nbsp;</p><p>Logos Circle: Lisbon continues to gather momentum, and we hosted the latest edition early in July. During the event, we moved forward with plans to establish a decentralised marketplace for the Talude grogue factory with plans to connect the family-run operation with local businesses and buyers. If you have ideas to help move this project forward, please join the discussion in the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.com/channels/973324189794697286/1376483221717651467">Logos Circle: Lisbon channel</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>We hosted the inaugural Logos Circle: London on 10 July. Attendees identified several issues facing the UK capital, such as a diminishing innovation culture, heavy reliance on outsourced skills while ignoring local talent, and the consequences of AI for employment. We look forward to the next one and developing ways to deploy Logos technologies to help the local community.</p><p>Later in July, the first Logos Circle: Zanzalu discussed the deep employment crisis stifling the prospects of the local community. Together, we explored ideas for new initiatives, including local p2p markets, regenerative green jobs, and web3-native skill networks. The event resulted in us working to support a local school with computing equipment and training. You can&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://donate.straighttraining.center/">support this initiative here</a>.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:4096/2886;" alt="logos-circle-zan.jpeg" src="/media-images/logos_circle_zan_94963d970e-c8bfc4cf-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_circle_zan_94963d970e-c8bfc4cf-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_circle_zan_94963d970e-c8bfc4cf-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="4096" height="2886" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Logos Circle: Zanzalu's first meeting.</figcaption></figure><p>We announced the first Logos Circle: Los Angeles will be hosted at the Arts District Brewing Company on 20 August. We invite anyone who’s excited about optimising how we cooperate to progress as a society to join us. We’ll be discussing the Logos technology stack and how communities can leverage it to tackle local challenges.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://lu.ma/vo03t8xj">More info</a>.&nbsp;</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:857/857;" alt="la-logos-circle.png" src="/media-images/la_logos_circle_c9bf8458e4-49315881-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/la_logos_circle_c9bf8458e4-49315881-750.webp 750w, /media-images/la_logos_circle_c9bf8458e4-49315881-857.webp 857w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="857" height="857" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure><p>We hosted the first Logos Circles: Online on 8 July via X Spaces, and have held another three since. The events are every Tuesday at 17:00 UTC. Logos Circles: Online provide a regular meeting space for those inspired by Logos’ mission and technologies to connect, support each other’s initiatives, and cultivate the power of the Logos grassroots movement worldwide. Join the spaces&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">via the Logos X account</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>Interested in Logos Circles but don’t see one in your area? You can help bring the movement to your community by starting a local Circle.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://discord.gg/logosnetwork">Join us on Discord</a> and let us know you're interested. We’ll support you with guidance, planning resources, and promotion.&nbsp;</p><p><strong>Parallel Society Festival and Road to PSF</strong></p><p>As preparations for&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://psf.logos.co/">next year’s PSF</a> continue in the background, we kicked off our Road to PSF promotional efforts in Zanzibar. Logos collaborated with Zanzalu to host an experiential event in Fumba Town, Zanzibar, featuring live performances from local musicians and DJs, alongside the Kwetu Kwenu Community Market, and much more.&nbsp;</p><p>These pop-up events around the world aim to connect global and local scenes through music, art, conversation, and technology and provide a taste of next year’s PSF. Look out for one near you, or if you have an idea for a potential location, please share it with us, and we can discuss how to make it happen.</p><ul><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://psf.logos.co/">Learn more about PSF</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://form.jotform.com/251343955577063">Volunteer at PSF</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://lu.ma/psf">Attend PSF</a></li><li><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">Follow Logos on X for updates about the next Road to PSF event</a></li></ul><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:2935/1636;" alt="logos-state-of-july.png" src="/media-images/logos_state_of_july_8e388b1a24-84d3e5b5-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_state_of_july_8e388b1a24-84d3e5b5-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_state_of_july_8e388b1a24-84d3e5b5-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="2935" height="1636" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Road to PSF events give a taste of next year’s Parallel Society Festival.&nbsp;</figcaption></figure><p><strong>Logos at Zanzalu</strong></p><p>Various Logos contributors supported the Zanzalu pop-up city initiative in the Fumba Free Economic Zone in Zanzibar. Logos contributor Václav shared his insights on vulnerabilities inherent to centralised infrastructure on 25 July as part of the technical portion of the event.</p><p>Logos also co-curated Zanzalu’s Student Weekend on 26 and 27 July, which focused on encouraging innovators from the area’s youth talent to inspire future generations to think creatively about community organisation.</p><figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:1920/1446;" alt="logos-student-weekend-zanzibar.jpeg" src="/media-images/logos_student_weekend_zanzibar_d376ea5ba2-f8209fd6-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_student_weekend_zanzibar_d376ea5ba2-f8209fd6-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_student_weekend_zanzibar_d376ea5ba2-f8209fd6-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1446" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Logos co-curated the Student Weekend alongside Zanzalu.</figcaption></figure><h3 id="learning">Learning</h3><p><strong>X Spaces and Podcasts</strong></p><p>Alongside the new Logos Circles: Online discussions, Logos hosts regular X Spaces with those at the forefront of governance and technological innovation.
