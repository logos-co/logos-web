12:T503f,<p>Technical highlights include Codex’s first testnet launch and Waku’s progress stabilising the protocols, which included a new rate-limiting system, RLNv2. On the movement-building front, we hosted the first annual Parallel Society Congress and expanded our community significantly with the launch of Logos Operators – a Bitcoin ordinals collection representing the founding members of our nascent cybernation.&nbsp;</p><p>Read on to learn more about Logos’ technical and movement-building achievements in 2024.</p><h2 id="technology-development">Technology development</h2><p>Technology is core to the Logos mission. The pioneering blockchain communities emerging throughout the crypto sector need robust infrastructure that does not rely on centralised chokepoints if they are to evolve into cyberstates capable of competing with nation-state institutions.</p><p>This year, we made great strides in terms of the Logos technology stack’s development. While significant research and development challenges remain, progress on the foundational technologies required for the thriving of post-state governmental systems continues to advance.</p><p>Below, we highlight the most significant technical breakthroughs of the last 12 months.</p><h3 id="waku">Waku</h3><p><img alt="Waku_EoY.png" src="/media-images/Waku_Eo_Y_a0ef8b6d81-ec67c357-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/Waku_Eo_Y_a0ef8b6d81-ec67c357-750.webp 750w, /media-images/Waku_Eo_Y_a0ef8b6d81-ec67c357-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1440" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Waku is the Logos stack’s decentralised messaging layer. It protects sensitive communications, enabling individuals to organise without fear of surveillance or deplatforming. Its modular architecture enables existing blockchain communities to integrate components according to their user needs and is already being used by <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://status.app/">Status</a>, The Graph, and Railgun.</p><p>This year, Waku made significant progress in improving the protocol’s reliability while exploring how to expand the network to work on more devices. A key highlight was the development and integration of an&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://blog.waku.org/2024-07-10-rln-v2-stateless-light-clients/">improved rate-limiting system</a>, RLNv2, which increases the network’s resilience against DoS attacks and spam.&nbsp;</p><p>Waku also improved the usability of edge nodes (light clients). Reductions in hardware and bandwidth requirements enable the protocols to run more efficiently on resource-restricted devices, like mobile phones. The protocols are integrated within the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://status.app/">Status Mobile application</a>, which can now run in “light mode” with significantly less bandwidth overhead than previous versions.&nbsp;</p><p>Waku’s blog provides a more <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://blog.waku.org/waku-2024-year-in-review/">detailed roundup of Waku’s 2024</a>.</p><h3 id="codex">Codex</h3><p><img alt="codex-blog-181224.png" src="/media-images/codex_blog_181224_2872073e0a-7ab11040-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/codex_blog_181224_2872073e0a-7ab11040-750.webp 750w, /media-images/codex_blog_181224_2872073e0a-7ab11040-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1280" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>Codex is the archiving layer of the Logos technology stack. It exists to safeguard the data of whoever needs durable data storage, including those nascent blockchain communities and the institutions of emerging cyberstates. Without confidence in and access to archival data, a community cannot preserve its most important cultural and administrative records, leading to instability and eventual collapse.&nbsp;</p><p>This year, Codex launched its&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://cointelegraph.com/press-releases/codex-testnet-launch-ramps-up-decentralised-data-storage-with-democratisation-and-incentivisation">public testnet</a> and released its&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://docs.codex.storage/learn/whitepaper">whitepaper</a> – both major roadmap milestones. This first public, non-incentivised testnet enables anyone to join the Codex network as a node operator, providing the Codex team with valuable insights about the network’s robustness under diverse conditions.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>Codex’s blog provides a more <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://blog.codex.storage/codex-2024-year-in-review/">detailed roundup of Codex’s 2024</a>.</p><h3 id="nomos">Nomos</h3><p><img alt="nomos-2024.png" src="/media-images/nomos_2024_5ac4750dd7-82954381-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/nomos_2024_5ac4750dd7-82954381-750.webp 750w, /media-images/nomos_2024_5ac4750dd7-82954381-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1200" height="800" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><br>Nomos is the consensus engine of the Logos technology stack. When released, it will provide blockchain infrastructure for those building onchain governmental, financial, and social institutions. The network’s architecture is designed to ensure that communities settling upon it retain maximum sovereignty while benefiting from the security of a network-wide shared validator set.</p><p>This year, Nomos made progress in finalising the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://blog.nomos.tech/why-we-chose-kzg-for-nomosda/">network’s data availability solution, NomosDA</a>. The three core data availability processes (encoding, verification, and reconstruction) have working implementations with very favourable performance times, and the network's dispersal, replication, and sampling protocols are running well in simulations. The team also designed and implemented Cryptarchia, a consensus protocol enabling the Nomos network to select block leaders using the private proof-of-stake consensus mechanism. Although Cryptarchia is sufficient for a v1 implementation, the team has identified various possible improvements for future prototype iterations.</p><p>These developments, plus progress on the Nomos Blendnet (formerly Mixnet), the network’s Coordination Layer, and its Native Zones, have enabled the team to build an early private Nomos testnet for internal testing purposes. In 2025, the focus will be on delivering the full specification of the Nomos network.</p><p>Nomos’ blog provides a more <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://blog.nomos.tech/2024-year-in-review/">detailed roundup of Nomos’ 2024</a>.</p><h2 id="building-a-movement">Building a movement</h2><p>Logos is about more than technology. To achieve the radical upgrade to human governance we believe blockchain and related technologies can underpin, we must nurture a core community of mission-committed individuals while building cutting-edge technology.</p><h3 id="events">Events</h3><p>Events give us an opportunity to spread our technologies and ideas and to connect with those most receptive to our vision of a post-state future.</p><p>The highlight of our events calendar was undoubtedly the first&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://psc.logos.co/">Parallel Society Congress</a> – a Logos-hosted gathering in Bangkok that saw thought leaders and pioneers come together, share their work, and forge vital connections with aligned projects.</p><p>The efforts of our fellow collaborators, including build_cities, Charter Cities Institute, Kleros, Funding the Commons, ZuVillage Georgia, Shan Hai Woo, RNS.ID, Women in Web3Privacy, DarkFi, and Railgun made PSC a massive success that we aim to recreate annually.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/Logos_network">Follow Logos on X</a> to stay up to date with PSC 2025 plans as we reveal them.</p><p><img alt="PSC-Logos.jpg" src="/media-images/PSC_Logos_b117d4bb4e-fa2a2688-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/PSC_Logos_b117d4bb4e-fa2a2688-750.webp 750w, /media-images/PSC_Logos_b117d4bb4e-fa2a2688-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1999" height="1125" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><br>In addition to PSC, Logos representatives spoke and delivered workshops at several key crypto conferences. They include:</p><ul><li>Logos and Waku representatives presented at Web3 Privacy Now’s ETHDam side event (12–14 April).</li><li>We cohosted the Dubai edition of Building Network States with build_cities (18 April).&nbsp;</li><li>Logos and Waku representatives spoke at the Web3 Privacy Now Summit in Prague (30 May).</li><li>Several contributors from across Logos and its technology stack presented and held workshops during this year’s EthCC (9–11 June).&nbsp;</li><li>Logos cofounder Jarrad Hope presented on how crypto offers a solution to the problem of&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/illicit-financial-flows-crypto-solution">illicit financial flows</a> at PoW Summit in Frankfurt (28–29 Sept), HCPP24 in Prague (4–6 Oct), and this year’s Devcon in Bangkok (14 Nov).&nbsp;</li><li>Jarrad also appeared on a privacy-focused panel at PoW Summit.</li><li>Codex hosted the Decentralised Data Summit the day before PSC in Bangkok.</li></ul><p><img alt="data-summit-codex.jpg" src="/media-images/data_summit_codex_155100f1c1-04c3aa72-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/data_summit_codex_155100f1c1-04c3aa72-750.webp 750w, /media-images/data_summit_codex_155100f1c1-04c3aa72-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1999" height="1487" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h3 id="logos-assemblies">Logos Assemblies</h3><p>Logos Assemblies are informal pop-up gatherings scattered around the world. We hosted the first Logos Assembly in Athens this April alongside our annual in-person working week.&nbsp;</p><p>Since LA 1: Athens, there have been two more Logos Assemblies: LA 2: Belgrade on 3 June and LA 3: Brno on 13 June. The gatherings provide an informal space in which to discuss the ideas and technologies driving the parallel society movement.&nbsp;</p><p>We look forward to seeing more Logos Assemblies emerge in 2025.</p><p><img alt="LA1-Athens.jpg" src="/media-images/LA_1_Athens_44e4409820-821ab9a8-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/LA_1_Athens_44e4409820-821ab9a8-750.webp 750w, /media-images/LA_1_Athens_44e4409820-821ab9a8-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h3 id="operators">Operators</h3><p>In Q2 2024, we announced Logos Operators – a Bitcoin ordinals collection representing the founders of the digital frontier we are colonising.&nbsp;</p><p>The Logos Operators’ journey began in May with an onchain statement – inscribing our&nbsp;<a href="/media/article/logos-a-declaration-of-independence-in-cyberspace">manifesto</a> in the then-largest Bitcoin block ever mined.&nbsp;</p><p>After our record-breaking Bitcoin inscription, Logos Operators launched&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://exit.logos.co/">The Exit</a> – an interactive series of quests, challenges, and tests to determine those deserving of becoming founding members of our movement.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>The collection officially launched on 16 December, with all 1,000 ordinals available in the first minting epoch claimed in just 13 minutes. Those holding a Logos Operator can now stake it over at the&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://dashboard.logos.co/operators/4892/">Logos Operator Dashboard</a> where they will receive XP that will continue to benefit them into the future as new utility for Operators comes online.</p><p>For more information about Logos Operators’, <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/exit_operator/status/1870209355708018931">check the project’s annual recap</a> and&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://x.com/exit_operator">follow the Exit Operator on X</a>.</p><p><img alt="psc-operators.jpg" src="/media-images/psc_operators_819690d700-cd8bd80d-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/psc_operators_819690d700-cd8bd80d-750.webp 750w, /media-images/psc_operators_819690d700-cd8bd80d-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1702" height="1134" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><h3 id="logos-cells">Logos Cells</h3><p>The first Logos Cells emerged in 2024. Logos Cells are decentralised groups of Logos Operators dedicated to building and nurturing local networks that advance the Logos mission.
