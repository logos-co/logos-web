1c:T4cec,<h2 id="cultivating-community">Cultivating community</h2><p>Logos is a movement committed to reshaping how people come together and organise. Achieving this vision depends on the dedication of an engaged, motivated community. If our&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/">principles</a> resonate with you, we invite you to contribute your skills and creativity. We recognise and support those who choose to build with us.</p><p>We’ve just launched a community contributions portal, which makes it easy to submit proposals to make technical and non-technical contributions to Logos (including writing for Logos Press Engine).&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://contribute.logos.co/en/">Check out the Logos Contribute portal</a> and start contributing to the Logos movement today!</p><h3 id="learning">Learning</h3><p><i><strong>Farewell to Westphalia</strong></i></p><p>We’re thrilled to reveal that&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i>, coauthored by Logos cofounder Jarrad Hope and author of&nbsp;<i>Crypto Anarchy, Cyberstates, and Pirate Utopias</i>, Peter Ludlow, will be available in paperback, ereader edition, and as a free download on 16 September.</p><p><img alt="logos-august-featured-farewell-to-westphalia.png" src="/media-images/logos_august_featured_farewell_to_westphalia_b62e0102e8-d3f34032-750.webp" srcset="/media-images/logos_august_featured_farewell_to_westphalia_b62e0102e8-d3f34032-750.webp 750w, /media-images/logos_august_featured_farewell_to_westphalia_b62e0102e8-d3f34032-1400.webp 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) calc(100vw - 32px), 700px" width="1600" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p><p>The book presents blockchain technology as infrastructure for a peaceful revolution in the way we organise and cooperate. It opens with a critique of the current nation-state system, arguing that new global issues are forcing nations to cede sovereignty to non-state entities. It then makes the case for blockchain’s potential to bring transparency, efficiency, and accountability to all levels of governance.&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://logos.co/farewell-to-westphalia/">Learn more</a>.&nbsp;</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FMGNN2ZT">Preorders for the ereader edition are live now</a>. We’re also appealing to the Logos community to help us get&nbsp;<i>Farewell to Westphalia</i> into legacy education institutions. If you have relevant contacts from universities or libraries, or if you know of any appropriate bookshops that might want to stock the book, please email&nbsp;<a href="mailto:pressengine@logos.co">pressengine@logos.co</a>.</p><div class="media-detail-video-frame"><div class="media-detail-video-aspect"><iframe title="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zI9zD9AEyqU&amp;t=1s" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/zI9zD9AEyqU" class="media-detail-video" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div></div><figure class="media"></figure><p><strong>Logos Press Engine</strong></p><p>We’re thrilled to see more community members contributing to Logos Press Engine. August’s Spaces tackled such topics as stablecoins, CBDCs, cognitive sovereignty, free media, and freethinking in cyberstates and blockchain communities, with widely known speakers in the liberty space and the crypto community.
