RFPs andLambda Prize

RFPs andLambda Prize

RFPs fund your team to build a specification. Lambda Prize rewards products on Logos that launch to real users. Choose your path.

Funded up front

RFP Programme

Send a proposal to a given spec. Get funded to build it.

RFPs provide funding to build and audit costly protocols, core building blocks and infrastructure. It starts with a spec from Logos. Your team responds with a proposal including approach, milestones, budget. Selected teams get a grant, paid as each milestone lands.

Lambda Prize

Launch a product. Find real users. Win the prize.

Lambda Prize rewards products that launch on the Logos stack and find real users. There is no application process. Develop in public and release early so usage grows as you build. Each prize sets its own success criteria and adoption target. Evaluators then confirm the product runs and the usage is real. First one to meet all the criteria wins that prize, paid in USDT.

Which one fits you?

Go to RFPs if…

  • You're an expert dev team with a track record of building protocols.
  • You want to learn the Logos ecosystem and one day run a product or business on it.
  • You want funding while you build that expertise.
Explore RFPs

Go toLambda Prize if…

  • You're a product and dev team and can grow a user base.
  • You want privacy and decentralisation at the core of your product.
  • You'd welcome a prize to kick off your search for market fit.

  1. 01

    Open source

    All work ships under MIT and Apache 2.0.

    Yours to run

    You publish and maintain what you build. Logos doesn't host or operate it.

  3. 03

    Real support

    Office hours, livestreams and direct access to the ecosystem development team.

Logos doesn't build the apps. You do.

The Logos stack is open source, delivering private-by-
default blockchain, storage, and messaging. What runs
on it is up to builders.

RFPs build the complex protocols and infrastructure on
top of Logos, while Lambda Prizes add an incentive to launch
apps with product-market fit.
