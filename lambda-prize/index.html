HOW IT WORKS

How it works.

  1. 01

    Pick a prize.

    Each prize lists what to build, the
    success criteria, and the payout.

  2. 02

    Talk to users.

    Find out what people would
    actually use before you write
    code.

  3. 03

    Plan and build.

    Build or reuse. If something
    already does the job, use it. You
    may not need to build much, or
    anything at all.

  4. 04

    Grow Adoption.

    Every prize requires real
    adoption. Release early so usage
    grows from day one.

  5. 05

    Claim it.

    Open a solution PR with
    evidence for each criterion.

  6. 06

    Get paid.

    The prize is paid in USDT when
    the submission is picked as the
    winner.

REQUIREMENTS

Real users and a
solution that works
for them.

REQUIREMENT 1

It’s used.

Each prize lists the adoption it needs. The LEZ Program Registry
prize asks for:

5
independent apps using your SDK
50
programs registered by 20+ authors over two months
40
public testimonials on Discord and X

REQUIREMENT 2

It works.

Your product does what the prize asks, for the people using it.
Build it, reuse what exists, or combine the two. Evaluators check
it against every success criterion.

Manufactured volume doesn’t count. Evaluators check account history and commit history.

THE RULES

The Rules

Five rules apply to every prize.

First valid solution wins.
Priority goes to the first solution PR that meets every criterion.
Pass or fail.
Evaluation feedback is pass or fail. For detail, ask evaluators in
#builder-hub.
Open source.
Solutions ship under MIT and Apache 2.0.
Open to anyone.
Any individual or team can enter. Logos contributors and their
contractors can’t.
Build in public.
It’s expected. Claiming someone else’s in-progress work isn’t.
